# SoulNote Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 11, 2026*

Welcome to **SoulNote**. SoulNote was built on a foundational promise: **your emotional life and private thoughts belong exclusively to you.** 

This Privacy Policy explains how SoulNote handles your information. In short: we do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any of your personal data.

---

## 1. Zero Cloud Data Collection & 100% Local Storage

* **Offline-First by Design**: All journal entries, recorded emotions, intensity levels, context tags, reflection answers, and personal notes are stored **strictly and solely on your device** inside your browser's local storage (`localStorage`).
* **No Remote Servers**: SoulNote has no backend user database, no cloud synchronization server, and no remote account system. Your entries never leave your device over the network.
* **No Accounts or Passwords**: You do not need an email, phone number, or account to use SoulNote. 

---

## 2. No Tracking, Analytics, or Advertising

* **Zero Telemetry**: We do not use Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Segment, or any other user tracking or behavioral telemetry software.
* **Zero Advertising SDKs**: SoulNote is 100% ad-free and contains no advertising trackers or third-party cookies.
* **Zero Third-Party Data Brokers**: We do not partner with data brokers, marketing agencies, or advertisers.

---

## 3. AI & Strict Data Minimization

SoulNote never submits your private journal entries, reflections, or personal diary notes to any external Artificial Intelligence (AI) models or Large Language Models (LLMs). Your writing is never used for model training, sentiment mining, or algorithmic tracking.

For the optional Mindful Perspective feature, SoulNote applies strict data minimization: only the short prompt topic title is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. Your actual written thoughts, reflections, and personal diary notes are strictly kept local on your device and are never transmitted.

---

## 4. Privacy & Security Features on Your Device

SoulNote provides built-in tools to help protect your journal from curious eyes on shared devices:

* **App Lock (PIN Protection)**: You can enable a 4-digit PIN lock within SoulNote Settings. When active, SoulNote locks immediately upon closing or switching away.
* **Biometric Authentication**: On supported devices, SoulNote can leverage your device's built-in biometric security (Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint) through standard browser WebAuthn capabilities.
* **Device Backups**: Your data is retained within your local browser storage. If you clear your browser's site data or cache, local entries will be erased unless you have exported a backup.

---

## 5. Data Ownership, Export & Total Deletion

You own 100% of your data at all times:

* **Export Entries (JSON)**: Download your complete journal history in open JSON format anytime directly from Settings or the footer.
* **Human-Readable Exports (TXT & PDF)**: Generate and save clean, printable text or formatted PDF copies of your entries client-side without any server processing.
* **Instant Data Wiping**: You can permanently and irrevocably erase all stored journal entries and reflections at any time via **Settings → Clear All Journal Data**. Once deleted locally, your data is gone forever because we have no copies on any server.

---

## 6. Progressive Web App (PWA) & Offline Use

When you install SoulNote as a Progressive Web App (PWA) to your home screen or desktop:
* The application runs locally via a lightweight client-side Service Worker.
* All cache files are stored locally in your browser's cache storage.
* SoulNote functions seamlessly without an active internet connection.

---

## 7. Changes to This Policy

Because SoulNote collects no user emails or contact details, any future updates to this policy will be reflected directly on this page with an updated timestamp. Given our architecture, our core commitment to zero cloud data collection will never change.

---
